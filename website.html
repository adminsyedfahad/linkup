About LinkUp

Built in Hyderabad. For Hyderabad.

Fahad
"The best food in our city comes from small kitchens that big apps forget. I built LinkUp to change that." — Fahad, Founder, LinkUp

LinkUp started with a simple question: why should the biryani place down the road, the chai café and the kirana next door struggle to get online? I saw local owners buried under big brands, paying high fees and waiting weeks for payments. So I built LinkUp — a delivery platform made only for them.

Every local shop in Hyderabad, one tap away.

A city where local businesses grow online, owners get paid fairly and on time, and customers get fresh food from their own neighbourhood.

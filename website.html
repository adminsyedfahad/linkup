"The best food in our city comes from small kitchens that big apps forget. I built LinkUp to change that." — Syed Fahad, Founder & CEO, LinkUp

LinkUp started with a simple question: why should the biryani place down the road, the chai café and the kirana next door struggle to get online? I saw local owners buried under big brands, paying high fees and waiting weeks for payments. So I built LinkUp — a delivery platform made only for them.

Every local shop in Hyderabad, one tap away.

A city where local businesses grow online, owners get paid fairly and on time, and customers get fresh food from their own neighbourhood.

    How It Works

    Ordering with confidence.

    How delivery, payments and cancellations actually work on LinkUp — in plain language.

    How delivery works

    • Your order goes to the nearest available LinkUp rider in your area.
    • You track your rider live on a map, from pickup all the way to your door.
    • Delivery is free within 5 km on orders above ₹149. Beyond that, the fee is calculated by distance and shown clearly before you pay — never added afterwards.

    Payments, cancellations & refunds

    • Payments are processed through a secure payment gateway — LinkUp never stores your card details.
    • Cancel free of charge any time before dispatch, even after the restaurant has accepted your order. The refund goes to your LinkUp wallet, usually within minutes.
    • Wrong item, missing item, or an order that never arrives? You get a full refund, plainly and always.

01

Commission & Fees

LinkUp charges partner restaurants and stores a flat 20% commission on the value of items sold through orders placed via LinkUp.

No upfront charges. There is no signup fee, no listing fee, and no monthly subscription to join LinkUp — you only pay commission on orders you actually receive.

The delivery fee shown to the customer is charged to the customer, not deducted from the partner's payout.

02

First Month Free

New partners get their first month on LinkUp commission-free, starting from the date your store goes live and starts accepting orders. Standard commission applies from the following billing cycle onward.

03

Weekly Settlement Cycle

Partner payouts are settled weekly. Each week's completed (delivered) orders are totalled, LinkUp's commission is deducted, and the net amount is paid out to the partner's registered bank account.

04

Partner Responsibilities

To keep the experience consistent for customers, every LinkUp partner is responsible for:

  • Accurate menu & prices: keeping items, descriptions and prices on LinkUp up to date and matching what's actually available in-store.
  • Realistic prep time: giving an honest estimate of how long an order takes to prepare, and accepting orders only when able to fulfil them within that time.
  • Hygiene: maintaining food safety and hygiene standards appropriate to a restaurant/store preparing food or packing groceries for delivery.
  • Packaging: packing orders securely enough to survive a delivery trip without spilling, leaking or being damaged.

LinkUp may pause or remove a partner that repeatedly falls short of these responsibilities, after reasonable notice.

05

Ending the Partnership

Either LinkUp or the partner may end the partnership at any time, for any reason, with reasonable written notice (email is sufficient). Orders already placed before the end date will still be fulfilled and settled under this policy.

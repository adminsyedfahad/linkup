01

First Order Discount

50% OFF your first order

Up to ₹100 off, on orders above ₹199. Applied automatically at checkout — no coupon code needed.

One per new customer. Eligibility is based on your account/phone number having no previous completed order on LinkUp, not on clearing your browser or app data.

02

Free Delivery

Free delivery up to 5 km

On orders above ₹149. Distance is measured in a straight line from the restaurant or store to your delivery address.

Orders below ₹149, or outside the 5 km radius, carry the standard distance-based delivery fee shown clearly before you pay.

03

Weekend Offers

20–50% OFF every weekend

Weekend discounts are set individually by each partner restaurant or store, not by LinkUp. The percentage, and which items it applies to, varies by store and can change week to week.

Weekend offers run on Saturday and Sunday only, and only for partners who have opted in that week.

04

Cashback

Cashback on every order

5% back in your LinkUp wallet, up to ₹50 per order. Credited after your order is marked delivered — not at the time of payment.

Wallet cashback can be used against a future order, up to the usual redemption limit at checkout.

05

LinkUp Points

Points on every order

5 points per order. 1 point = ₹1. Points can be used for up to 50% of your bill at checkout.

06

Buy 1 Get 1 (Selected Items)

Buy 1 Get 1 offers are being prepared with select restaurant and store partners and are not live yet. When enabled, they will apply only to specific items chosen by that partner, clearly marked on the item itself — never store-wide.

07

General Terms

  • Offers cannot be combined beyond the order in which they are described above (for example, the first-order discount and a weekend discount can both apply to the same order, in that order).
  • All discounts apply to the item total and do not reduce the delivery fee unless stated otherwise.
  • LinkUp reserves the right to limit, modify, suspend or end any offer, for any customer or partner, at any time.
  • Any attempt to abuse or game an offer (including creating multiple accounts) may result in the offer being reversed and, in serious cases, the account being suspended.

Offers may change or end at any time. The current, up-to-date terms are always the ones shown on this page.

01

Payments

Online payments on LinkUp are processed through a secure, PCI-compliant payment gateway. LinkUp itself never sees or stores your full card number, CVV or expiry date — that information is handled entirely by the payment gateway.

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available in select areas as an alternative to paying online.

02

Cancelling Before Dispatch

You can cancel your order free of charge, at any time before it is dispatched for delivery — this includes after the restaurant or store has already accepted the order and started preparing it.

The refund goes straight to your LinkUp wallet, usually within minutes.

03

Food Orders After Dispatch

Once a food order has been dispatched — meaning a rider has picked it up and is on the way to you — it cannot be cancelled.

If a dispatched food order is cancelled anyway (for example, the customer is unreachable or refuses delivery), the order is returned to the restaurant and no refund is given, because the food cannot be resold or reused.

04

Grocery & Non-Perishables After Dispatch

Grocery and non-perishable items (packaged goods, household items, and similar) can still be cancelled after dispatch, for as long as the rider is on the way and hasn't yet handed the order over.

Fresh items are excluded from this — fruit, vegetables, dairy, meat, and other perishables cannot be cancelled once dispatched, for the same reason as food orders: they cannot be resold.

Item typeCancel after dispatch?
Food (restaurant orders)Not possible
Fresh produce, dairy, meat & other perishablesNot possible
Grocery & non-perishable itemsYes, while the rider is still on the way
05

Wrong, Missing or Never-Arrived Orders

If your order arrives wrong, with missing items, or never arrives at all, you get a full refund — no exceptions. This applies regardless of item type, and regardless of whether the order was dispatched.

Report this from your order in the app, or email us, as soon as you notice — the sooner we hear from you, the faster we can put it right.

06

How Refunds Are Paid

  • Pre-dispatch cancellations: credited to your LinkUp wallet, usually within minutes.
  • Wrong/missing/never-arrived orders: credited to your LinkUp wallet or original payment method, typically within 3–5 business days once confirmed.
  • Wallet balance can be used toward your next order, or its redemption is subject to the usual checkout limits.
07

Contact Us

For any order issue, open the order inside the LinkUp app and use the support option there, or email adminlinkup@gmail.com with your order ID.
